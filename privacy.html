Privacy Policy — SRD Status Check & Payment Dates

Last updated: 9 November 2026

SRD Status Check is an independent information app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by SASSA, the Department of Social Development, or the South African government.

The short version

This app has no server. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you type into it is transmitted anywhere. Everything you enter stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

We do not collect, receive, transmit, sell or share any of it. We could not look up your grant status even if you asked us to — only SASSA can see that.

Why the app asks for your ID number

For one reason: so it can type the same two numbers into SASSA's own form for you, and check your ID for typos before you apply. A mistyped ID is the most common cause of an "identity verification failed" decline.

You do not have to provide it. Every other part of the app works without it.

The official SASSA portal inside the app

When you tap to check your status, the app opens SASSA's own website (srd.sassa.gov.za) in your phone's own browser — not inside the app.

  • Your connection is directly between your phone and SASSA. It does not pass through us.
  • Because the browser is run by your phone and not by this app, the app cannot see what you type there, what SASSA shows you, or your result. This is enforced by your phone's operating system, not merely promised by us.
  • The address bar you see is drawn by your phone, so you can always check you are on the real sassa.gov.za address.
  • The app offers to copy your saved ID and phone number to the clipboard so you can paste them. It cannot type them in for you, and it cannot submit anything.

SASSA's own privacy terms apply to anything you do on their site.

The other places the app can send you

The contact screen also lists SASSA's two other official channels. Tapping either hands you over to a different app on your phone. In both cases the app sends nothing about you, and it cannot see what happens once you are there.

  • SASSA's SRD WhatsApp line (082 046 8553). This opens WhatsApp, which is run by Meta — not by SASSA, and not by us. Anything you send there is between you, Meta and SASSA, under their privacy terms rather than this policy. It is listed because it uses far less data than the portal and often works when the portal is overloaded.
  • The toll-free helpline (0800 60 10 11). This opens your phone's dialler with the number filled in. The app does not place the call; you do.

Neither happens in the background. Both happen only because you tapped them.

The one thing the app downloads

On opening, the app checks srdstatustool.co.za/app-config.json — a small public file we publish — for corrections to payment dates and for any notice we need to show you. It is a plain request for a file. It sends nothing about you: no ID number, no phone number, no identifier of any kind. If your phone is offline the app simply uses the dates built into it.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, they are scheduled and delivered entirely by your phone. They are not push notifications, no server is involved, and we are not told that you received one.

Children

The app is intended for adults applying for or receiving a South African social grant and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because we never receive your information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. You are in full control:

  • My details → Delete my details removes your ID and phone from the keychain.
  • Settings → Delete everything removes everything the app has stored.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of it.
