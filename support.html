UNOFFICIAL — NOT A SASSA APP

Support — SRD Status Check

SRD Status Check is an independent app. It is not made by, for, or endorsed by SASSA, the Department of Social Development, or the South African government.

First, the important one

This app cannot check, change, approve, speed up or appeal your grant. It has no connection to SASSA and no access to your application. Only SASSA can see or decide anything about your grant.

If your question is about your actual grant — a declined month, a missing payment, wrong banking details, a status you do not understand — you need SASSA, not us:

  • 0800 60 10 11 — the toll-free helpline, free from any South African number
  • srd.sassa.gov.za — the official portal, the only place to apply, check and appeal
  • srd.sassa.gov.za/appeals — appeals, within 90 days of a decline

Common questions

The status check will not open

The portal is run by SASSA, not by this app. It is often overloaded in the first days of the month, and it frequently refuses connections from outside South Africa. The WhatsApp line on 082 046 8553 uses less data and often works when the website does not.

The app asks for my ID number — why?

So it can fill the same two numbers into SASSA's own form for you, and check your ID for typos before you apply. It is stored in your phone's keychain and never leaves the device. You do not have to provide it; every other part of the app works without it.

Are the payment dates official?

They are calculated from the published SASSA schedule and the real South African public holiday calendar. SASSA publishes the exact calendar each month — always confirm it there.

Are the grant amounts right?

They are based on published SASSA figures at the time of the last update, and they change. Always confirm with SASSA before relying on a number.

The app is in my language but a guide is in English

The app interface is translated into six languages. The 40 long-form guides are written in English, and that is marked in the app.

Something wrong with the app itself

Bugs, crashes, a wrong date, a bad translation: heshventures123@gmail.com

Please say which phone you are using and which language the app is set to. We cannot help with your grant itself — see above.
