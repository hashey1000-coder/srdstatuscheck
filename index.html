UNOFFICIAL — NOT A SASSA APP

SRD Status Check & Payment Dates

An unofficial helper for the SASSA SRD R370 grant. Not made by, for, or endorsed by SASSA or any government department.

What it does

  • Your next payment date, worked out on your phone from the real working-day schedule and South African public holidays — no data needed
  • What each portal status actually means, and the right next step for each one
  • Nine calculators: SRD eligibility, back-pay, which grant you qualify for, and more
  • Appeal deadline tracking, 90 days from a decline
  • 40 guides stored on the phone
  • Six languages: English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Sesotho and Sepedi

Privacy

No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads. The app has no server. Anything you enter stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall it.
